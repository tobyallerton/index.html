# Car Deal Finder (browser extension)

Saves the cars you scroll past on eBay UK and Facebook Marketplace, values each one against similar cars, tags deals right on the page, and notifies you when a new one appears.

Works in Edge, Brave, Chrome and other Chromium browsers.

## Install (about 2 minutes)

1. Download `car-deal-finder-extension.zip` and unzip it. Keep the folder somewhere permanent, like Documents. The browser loads it from there.
2. Open the extensions page: `edge://extensions` in Edge, or `brave://extensions` in Brave.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (a toggle on the left in Edge, top right in Brave).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped `car-deal-finder-extension` folder.
5. Pin the icon so it's always visible: click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Car Deal Finder.

The dashboard opens on install. Click the icon any time to open it again.

## Use

1. In the dashboard, type a car and price range, then open **eBay · newest**, **eBay · sold prices**, or **Facebook Marketplace** (log in to Facebook as normal).
2. Scroll through the results. A dark "◆ cars on this page" button at the bottom right shows it's working, and every listing gets a tag such as "STEAL · 29% under (£1,210)".
3. A model gets valuations once at least 4 of it are saved. Scrolling the eBay sold-prices search for the same car makes them much more accurate.

## Updating

Replace the folder's files with the new version, then click the reload arrow on the extension's card in the extensions page and refresh any open eBay or Facebook tabs.
