What it reads

Only the eBay and Facebook pages you open yourself. Nothing is sent anywhere: listings stay in this browser.

Red flags

Cheap usually means something. Cat S/N, spares or repair, no MOT, swaps, "deposit" prices and £1 placeholders are flagged so they don't top the list.

Auctions

An eBay auction shows the current bid, not the final price. It's marked, gets no alert, and isn't used to value other cars.

Always verify

Check the MOT history on gov.uk and run an HPI check before you pay. Valuations come from asking prices and are a starting point.
