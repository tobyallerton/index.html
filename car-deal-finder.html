Why a bookmark button?

Facebook has no public API for Marketplace, and auto-scraping breaks its rules and gets accounts blocked. The button only reads what's already on your screen.

Red flags

Cheap usually means something. Cat S/N, spares or repair, no MOT, swaps, "deposit" prices and placeholder £1 listings are flagged so they don't top the list.

Auctions

An eBay auction shows the current bid, not the final price, so it's marked and left out of other cars' valuations until it sells.

Always verify

Check the MOT history on gov.uk and run an HPI check before you pay. These numbers come from asking prices and are a starting point.
